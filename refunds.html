REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Appointments and refunds

Choose your appointment carefully. Your booking is confirmed only after payment is verified and the confirmation page reports success. A selected slot is held temporarily while payment is completed. The payment session expires according to the payment provider’s rules; an unpaid or expired session does not confirm an appointment.

Refunds when we cancel

Refunds are available only for orders cancelled by ADITYA CONSULTANTS, except where a refund or other remedy is required by applicable law. Approved refunds will be returned to the original payment source and should reflect in that account within two working days. Posting times may depend on the payment provider or the customer’s bank.

Customer cancellations and rescheduling

Consultation fees are final and non-refundable if a customer cancels, requests a reschedule, does not attend, or is dissatisfied with the consultation, except where a refund or other remedy is required by applicable law. Please check the market, appointment date and time before paying.

When this policy applies

This policy applies to orders placed after it is published on this website. For orders placed earlier, the terms shown at the time of purchase apply, together with any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Payment and cancellation help

For a payment or booking issue, email adityamalik@atomicmail.io with your name and booking reference. Do not send card details by email.
