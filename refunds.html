REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Appointments and refunds

Consultation requests are not bookings and no payment is taken through this website. An appointment is confirmed only after we agree a time and scope with you in writing.

Final and non-refundable

All consultation fees are final and non-refundable once payment has been made, including where a client cancels, reschedules, does not attend, or is dissatisfied with the consultation. Please check the appointment date, time, scope and price before paying. This policy does not limit any refund or other right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Before payment

You may withdraw an unconfirmed consultation request at any time by emailing adityamalik@atomicmail.io. There is no charge for an unconfirmed request. We will agree the appointment details with you before asking for payment.

Contact about an appointment

For questions, email us with your name and appointment date. Do not send payment-card details by email.
