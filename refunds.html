REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Appointments and refunds

Choose your appointment carefully. Your booking is confirmed only after payment is verified and the confirmation page reports success. A selected slot is held temporarily while payment is completed. The payment session expires according to the payment provider’s rules; an unpaid or expired session does not confirm an appointment.

Final and non-refundable

All consultation fees are final and non-refundable once payment has been made, including where a client cancels, requests a reschedule, does not attend, or is dissatisfied with the consultation. Please check the market, appointment date and time before paying. This policy does not limit any refund or other right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Payment and cancellation help

INR payments are processed through Cashfree’s hosted checkout and GBP payments through Razorpay’s hosted checkout. For a payment or booking issue, email adityamalik@atomicmail.io with your name and booking reference. Do not send card details by email.
