Information collected for bookings

To arrange and administer a consultation, the booking form collects your name, email, selected market, appointment date and time, and optionally your phone number and business name. The booking system also stores a booking reference, payment-link reference and payment status. The general enquiry form instead prepares a draft email in your email application.

How we use it

We use booking details to check availability, hold your selected slot, create and verify the payment request, confirm the appointment, and respond to you. We do not ask for passwords, card numbers, bank credentials, government ID or confidential business documents in the booking form.

Payment processing

When you continue to payment, we send your name, email and optional phone number to Razorpay to create a hosted payment request. Payment instrument details are entered on Razorpay and are not collected or stored by this website. Razorpay processes payment information under its own terms and privacy notice. We receive payment and booking status needed to confirm your appointment.

Service providers and storage

Vercel hosts this website and its booking functions. Upstash Redis stores booking details and appointment holds. Pending booking records expire after up to seven days; confirmed booking records are retained for 90 days after the appointment. Razorpay separately maintains payment records under its policies and applicable requirements. Your email provider handles messages you choose to send from your own email application.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete booking information, email adityamalik@atomicmail.io. We may need to retain information where required for legal, accounting or payment-dispute purposes. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and law.

Cookies and external services

This site does not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser connects to Vercel to load the site and to Razorpay if you proceed to hosted payment.
