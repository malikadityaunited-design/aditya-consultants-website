# ADITYA CONSULTANTS website

Static Vercel site for Aditya Malik, trading as ADITYA CONSULTANTS. India and the UK are distinct markets, with cross-border advisory as an additional service. Includes consultation pricing, business intake, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Refund/Cancellation pages.

## Enquiries and consultations

The business intake form asks for contact information and limited business context to prepare for an initial consultation. It opens the visitor's email application with a draft addressed to adityamalik@atomicmail.io. The visitor must review and send the email; the website does not transmit or store submissions.

Initial consultations are approximately 45 minutes. Prices shown: INR 1,500 for India and GBP 50 for the United Kingdom. Fees are final and non-refundable, subject to any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. The site does not currently accept payment or confirm appointments online. Confirm appointment availability, format, applicable taxes and payment instructions before accepting payment.

## Razorpay review

Legal name, trade name, business start date, contact address and phone are shown on the About and Contact pages. Keep these details consistent with the Razorpay account and supporting records.

Do not claim international payments are enabled until Razorpay approves activation. This website does not itself secure approval or integrate payment processing. Requirements and eligibility can vary by merchant; confirm them in the Razorpay Dashboard and current official guidance.

## Vercel deployment

Import the GitHub repository in Vercel. Select Other as framework preset, leave the build command empty, and set the output directory to the current folder. Deploy; Vercel displays the URL on success.
